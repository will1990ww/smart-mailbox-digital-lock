HDB letterbox FAQ

Do I need HDB, town council or MCST approval to change my letterbox lock?

Generally, changing the lock on your own unit's letterbox with a like-for-like fit does not need approval, as long as the letterbox itself is not altered. Rules can differ by estate, so check with your town council or MCST if you are unsure. For a block-wide or common-area mailbox bank, the MCST, managing agent or town council must approve the works first, and we help prepare that request before anything is scheduled.

Does the town council or HDB change letterbox locks?

The lock on your own letterbox is yours to maintain, so residents normally arrange the replacement themselves. For block-wide mailbox works, the town council decides and we can help prepare the request.

When should I change a letterbox lock that still works?

When you move into a resale flat, when a tenant or domestic helper leaves, after a key goes missing, or if mail has been tampered with. Anyone who kept a key or knows the code can still open the box. A battery-free code lock (from S$50) lets you change the code instead of the lock next time.

How much does a letterbox lock installation cost in Singapore?

Supply and installation starts from S$50 for a standard HDB or condo letterbox. If the mailbox is locked and the key is lost, opening adds S$25 per unit, so a typical open-and-replace job starts at S$75. Digital touch-PIN models start at S$60 and the range tops out at S$85. Urgent installation adds S$10. The final price is confirmed in writing after we review your photo.
