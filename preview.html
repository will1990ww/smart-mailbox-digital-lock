Before you book

Compatibility, timing, warranty & pricing

Useful details so you know exactly what to expect — no surprises on the day.

Which mailboxes are compatible?

We fit the vast majority of standard HDB and condominium letterbox doors, including horizontal and vertical layouts. Send a photo of the door front and the inside latch and we confirm compatibility before quoting.

Typical installation time

Most single replacements take 15–30 minutes on site once the mailbox is accessible. Bulk jobs are scheduled as one coordinated visit to minimise disruption.

Mailboxes we may not be able to service

Severely rusted or deformed doors, non-standard custom enclosures, or mailboxes owned and sealed by a third party may need the managing agent's approval or a door repair first. We tell you upfront if yours is unsuitable.

Warranty coverage

Selected installed models include a 1-year workmanship warranty covering fitting and mechanism defects. Wear from misuse, forced entry or lost user-set codes is not covered. Full terms are on the Warranty page.

How to choose a lock

Prefer no batteries and no keys? Choose a mechanical code lock. Want a physical backup? Choose password + backup key. Want a touch PIN? Choose a smart model. We help you pick based on your mailbox and habits.

What affects the final price

The starting price assumes a compatible, accessible mailbox. Lost-key opening, damaged locks, non-standard doors and bulk quantities affect the final figure, which is always confirmed in writing before work begins.

Frequently asked questions

Letterbox lock questions, answered

How much does a letterbox lock installation cost in Singapore?

Standard supply and installation starts from S$50. Lost-key unlocking starts from S$25. Our range goes up to S$85 for the premium 4-digit zinc-alloy model. The final price is confirmed in writing after you send a photo of your mailbox.

Do you service HDB and condo letterboxes islandwide?

Yes. We provide islandwide Singapore service for both HDB and condominium letterboxes, including same-day appointments where available.

I lost my letterbox key. Can you help?

Yes. We can open a locked letterbox and replace the lock. Lost-key opening starts from S$25 and proof that you are the resident, owner or authorised person is required on site before we open any mailbox.

How long does a letterbox lock replacement take?

Most standard installations take 15 to 30 minutes once we are on site and the mailbox is accessible.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash, PayNow and bank transfer. A written quotation is always provided before any work begins.

Do you offer smart or keyless letterbox locks?

Yes. We install touch-PIN and horizontal smart letterbox locks from S$60, plus battery-free mechanical code locks from S$50, with fingerprint and app models coming soon.

Do you install smart or digital letterbox locks?

Yes. We supply and install electronic smart digital letterbox locks with a touch PIN, such as our Smart Battery Lock (P4) and Horizontal Smart Lock (P7) from S$60, plus battery-free digital combination locks from S$50. These are mailbox locks for HDB and condo letterboxes, not main-door digital locks.

What is the difference between a combination, keyless, PIN and digital letterbox lock?

They overlap. A combination or code lock uses a mechanical dial with no battery. A keyless lock simply means no physical key. A PIN or digital lock uses an electronic touch keypad and a small battery. We help you pick the right one for your mailbox and habits.

Do you sell main-door HDB digital locks?

No. We specialise only in letterbox and mailbox locks. If you are searching for a digital lock for your flat's front door, that is a different product. For a smart or digital lock for your letterbox, our range from S$50 to S$85 is exactly what you need.
