How to choose in about thirty seconds

Most people over-think this. Three questions narrow nine models to one or two.

Do you want to stop carrying a key? If yes, that rules out P1. Do you want to avoid batteries entirely? If yes, that rules out P4 and P7, leaving seven mechanical options. Do you need a way back in if the code is forgotten? If yes, skip P2 — P3, P5, P6, P8 and P9 all ship with a backup key that retrieves your code.

If the mailbox is outdoors or in a coastal estate, weight the zinc-alloy builds (P6 and P9). If an elderly resident will use it daily, P5 exists specifically for that.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions

How much does a letterbox lock installation cost in Singapore?

Supply and installation starts from S$50 for a standard HDB or condo letterbox. If the mailbox is locked and the key is lost, opening adds S$25 per unit, so a typical open-and-replace job starts at S$75. Digital touch-PIN models start at S$60 and the range tops out at S$85. Urgent installation adds S$10. The final price is confirmed in writing after we review your photo.

Is there a letterbox lock that does not need a battery?

Yes — most of our range. Our combination models (P2, P3, P5, P6, P8 and P9) have no battery at all, so there is nothing to charge and nothing to fail. Only the digital touch-PIN models (P4 and P7) use a replaceable battery, and those warn you before it runs low.

Can you match the existing lock on my letterbox?

In most cases, yes. Send one straight-on photo of the mailbox front and we identify the cut-out, cam type and swivel direction, then confirm in writing which model fits. If we confirm a model and it does not fit on site, you pay nothing — including no attendance fee.

How long does a letterbox lock replacement take?

Most single replacements take 15 to 30 minutes once we are on site and the mailbox is accessible. Bulk jobs for a whole block are scheduled as one coordinated visit.
