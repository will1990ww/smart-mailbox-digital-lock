Questions about our locks

How much does a letterbox lock installation cost in Singapore?

Supply and installation starts from S$50 for a standard HDB or condo letterbox. If the mailbox is locked and the key is lost, opening adds S$25 per unit, so a typical open-and-replace job starts at S$75. Digital touch-PIN models start at S$60 and the range tops out at S$85. Urgent installation adds S$10. The final price is confirmed in writing after we review your photo.

Digital letterbox lock or combination lock: which should I choose?

A digital touch-PIN lock is quicker in a dim corridor and accepts a longer PIN, but it runs on a coin battery. A mechanical combination lock never needs a battery, so it suits anyone who wants to fit it and forget it, and it is the better choice for mailboxes exposed to rain. Seven of our nine models are battery-free.

Is there a letterbox lock that does not need a battery?

Yes, most of our range. The combination models P2, P3, P5, P6, P8 and P9, plus the keyed P1, have no battery at all. Only the digital touch-PIN models P4 and P7 use a replaceable coin battery, and they warn you before it runs low.

Can you match the existing lock on my letterbox?

In most cases, yes. Send one straight-on photo of the mailbox front and we identify the cut-out, cam type and turning direction, then confirm in writing which model fits. If we confirm a model and it does not fit on site, you pay nothing, including no attendance fee.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. Every lock we supply and install carries a 1-year warranty covering the lock and our installation. If it fails under normal use within a year, we come back and fix or replace it at no charge, with no call-out fee. Keep the WhatsApp thread from your job; it is your proof of purchase.
