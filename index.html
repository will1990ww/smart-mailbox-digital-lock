    Frequently asked questions

    Letterbox lock questions, answered

    How much does a letterbox lock installation cost in Singapore?

    Supply and installation starts from S$50 for a standard HDB or condo letterbox. If the mailbox is locked and the key is lost, opening adds S$25 per unit, so a typical open-and-replace job starts at S$75. Digital touch-PIN models start at S$60 and the range tops out at S$85. Urgent installation adds S$10. The final price is confirmed in writing after we review your photo.

    Digital letterbox lock or combination lock — which should I choose?

    A digital touch-PIN lock is quicker in a dim corridor and accepts a longer PIN, but it runs on a coin battery. A mechanical combination lock never needs a battery, so it suits anyone who wants to fit it and forget it, and is the better choice for mailboxes exposed to rain. Seven of our nine models are battery-free.

    Are you really open 24 hours?

    Yes. We answer WhatsApp and phone round the clock, every day of the year, including public holidays. Late-night and early-morning lost-key openings are a regular part of what we do. Urgent installation, at any hour, carries a S$10 surcharge, stated in writing before we travel — there is no separate night rate and no emergency multiplier.

    Do you service HDB and condo letterboxes islandwide?

    Yes. We cover every HDB town and condominium in Singapore, plus landed homes and outdoor mailboxes. We plan routes across the island each day, so your job is slotted into the next run passing your postal sector and you get a confirmed arrival window before we travel.

    I lost my letterbox key. Can you help?

    Yes. We open the locked mailbox and fit a replacement lock the same visit. Opening starts from S$25 per unit and we verify on site that you are the resident, owner, tenant or an authorised person before anything is opened. ID is sighted only, never copied.

    When do I pay?

    After the job is done and you have confirmed it. We install the lock, send you a short video of it working, and you only pay once you are satisfied — by PayNow, cash or bank transfer. There is no deposit, nothing to pay online and no card details taken.

    Is there a letterbox lock that does not need a battery?

    Yes — most of our range. Our combination models (P2, P3, P5, P6, P8 and P9) and the keyed P1 have no battery at all, so there is nothing to charge and nothing to fail. Only the digital touch-PIN models (P4 and P7) use a replaceable battery, and those warn you before it runs low.

    How long does a letterbox lock replacement take?

    Most single replacements take 15 to 30 minutes once we are on site and the mailbox is accessible. Bulk jobs for a whole block are scheduled as one coordinated visit.

    Do I need HDB, town council or MCST approval to change my letterbox lock?

    For your own unit's letterbox you do not normally need approval — the lock is yours to maintain, as long as it is a like-for-like fit and the letterbox itself is not altered. For a block-wide or common-area mailbox bank, the MCST, managing agent or town council must approve the works first, and we help prepare that request before anything is scheduled.

    Can you match the existing lock on my letterbox?

    In most cases, yes. Send one straight-on photo of the mailbox front and we identify the cut-out, cam type and swivel direction, then confirm in writing which model fits. If we confirm a model and it does not fit on site, you pay nothing — including no attendance fee.

    Do you charge a call-out fee?

    No call-out fee and no attendance fee. Standard visits on our daily route carry no travel charge at all. The only additional charge is S$10 for urgent installation, a special arrangement outside our planned route — and that figure is in your written quote before we travel.

    Do you work with interior designers and new construction projects?

    Yes. We are a one-stop letterbox lock solution for interior designers, renovation firms, developers, MCSTs, managing agents, town councils, property agents and landlords, as well as individual residents. Projects get one consistent model across every unit, a written per-unit scope, work scheduled around your handover or TOP date, and a single consolidated invoice.
