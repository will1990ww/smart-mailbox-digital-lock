Landed & outdoor

Mailbox lock replacement for landed homes and outdoor boxes

A mailbox mounted at a gate, on a pillar or free-standing in the open is a different engineering problem from a sheltered HDB letterbox bank — and it needs a different lock.

Why outdoor mailboxes eat locks

Singapore gives an exposed mailbox lock the worst of everything: sustained high humidity, driving rain on the monsoon side, and direct afternoon sun that drives moisture in and out of the mechanism every day. A lock that lasts a decade in a sheltered corridor can seize within eighteen months on an exposed gate post.

Coastal estates are harder still. Salt accelerates corrosion on the cam and the retaining hardware long before the visible face shows anything.

What we fit outdoors

For exposed positions we steer strongly towards the zinc-alloy bodies — P6 at S$65 and P9 at S$85. Both are mechanical, so there is no battery compartment for water to find.

We generally advise against the digital touch-PIN models in a fully exposed position. They are excellent in a sheltered corridor or a covered porch, but a keypad taking direct rain is a maintenance problem you do not need.
